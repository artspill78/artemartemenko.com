About

Artem Artemenko is a London-based author and memoirist, and the founder of Be Good To People, an independent publishing imprint. His writing draws on lived experience to explore betrayal, injustice, resilience, and forgiveness — and the belief that the human spirit retains the capacity to overcome and thrive, even through adversity.

His debut memoir, London Life: Be Good To People, recounts a personal journey through the streets of London. His second book, Moscow Life: Weaving Dreams into Reality, turns that same lens on Moscow — a city where ancient heritage and modern ambition converge.

He also writes a newsletter and blog on Substack.
